About Me

Hi! My name is Owen and I am an independent watchmaker, focusing on the servicing, restoration and care of mechanical timepieces. I am self-taught and have been obsessed with this craft for over 3 years now. It started with working on my own watches. Over time (no pun intended), this extended to restoring and servicing my friends' timepieces, and subsequently friends of friends.

What I hope separates me from others is not only the quality of my work, but also the portrayal of the stories behind the watches I work on. I try to create a detailed outline of the work done to restore the watch, and also try my best to portray a story of the watch itself.

What I Do

I currently specialise in mechanical watches, working on both Japanese and Swiss movements. I do not currently work on quartz repairs or battery changes.

  • Mechanical watch servicing
  • Vintage watch restoration
  • Movement cleaning and inspection
  • Mechanical troubleshooting
  • Regulation and adjustment

What I Work On

  • Swiss + Japanese movements
  • Manual wind + automatic movements
  • 2 hand and 3 hand movements
  • Small seconds hand
  • Day + date complication

Have a Watch in Need of Attention?

If you have a mechanical watch that needs servicing, restoration or simply some attention, I'd be happy to discuss it with you.
